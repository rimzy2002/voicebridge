Day 7 should be different from the previous six days. Instead of adding a large amount of new material, it should function as a **Week 1 Review + Communication Challenge + Progress Report**.

The learner should finish Day 7 knowing exactly what improved, what is still weak, and what the system will prioritize in Week 2.

# Day 7 — Week 1 Communication Challenge

**Main objective:** Review and test the skills from Days 1–6 in realistic speaking situations.

**Target outcome:** The learner should complete a full communication challenge combining fluency, structure, conversation, storytelling, vocabulary, listening, and pronunciation.

**Recommended duration:** 40–50 minutes
**Quick version:** 15–20 minutes

Core message:

> **Today is not about learning more. It is about proving what you can already do better than seven days ago.**

# Day 7 — Measure, Review and Personalize

For **Week 2**, I would increase the challenge substantially. A strong Day 8 would be **“Express Opinions and Disagree Naturally”**—teaching learners how to state an opinion, support it, partially agree, politely disagree, challenge ideas without sounding rude, and participate in short debates. That would begin the transition from basic conversational confidence toward genuinely advanced communication.
